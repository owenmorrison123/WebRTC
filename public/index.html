Campus Portal

A live video window between Evansville and Harlaxton. Set this screen up once, then press Start portal.

1Where is this screen?

2Enter the room key

Both campuses use the same key. Ask whoever set up the portal if you don’t have it.

3Check your camera and microphone

Mic level

Say something. The bar should move when you talk.

4Choose how it looks

You can change the frame any time later with the Frame button, even during a call.

Turning a TV on its side? If the computer can set its display to portrait, do that and leave rotation off. If it can't, choose Portrait and the rotation that makes the picture upright. You can also press R in the portal to try each one. On a normal landscape screen, Portrait shows a centered preview of how the portal will look.

The portal opens full screen and connects by itself as soon as the other campus is on. Bookmark the next page to skip this setup next time.

Advanced settings

Both screens follow the same Evansville-time schedule, so they sleep and wake together. 7:00 PM – 5:00 AM in Evansville is 1:00 AM – 11:00 AM at Harlaxton.
